Debates measures for pacification of southern Indians; authorizes postal contracts; receives re port on foreign loans. Orders report on formation of “a Confederacy with the powers of Europe” against the Barbary States; instructs Jefferson on consular conven tion with France. Debates southern Indian affairs. Fails to achieve quorum. Accepts South Carolina land cession; receives report on northern Indian affairs. Fails to achieve quorum. Fails to achieve quorum. Receives report of the Philadelphia Constitu tional Convention. Reelects treasury commissioners Arthur Lee, Walter Livingston, and Samuel Osgood; reduces civil list. Accepts John Adams’ retirement (post February 24, 1788); receives report on Netherlands protest. Debates Constitution submitted by Philadelphia Convention. Resolves to submit Constitution to the states. Receives report on prize money received by John Paul Jones; receives report on 1787 requisition.
