I sup pose I shall leave this City about Thursday next. I have submitted to the Bd. of Treasury the State and circumstances of the Bonds in Carolina, 3 and they have advised that the collections be facilitated by the rect. of paper money although there may be a loss sustained on it, rather than incur the inconveniences & losses by insol vencies, which would attend our attempts to get those debts paid in specie. I have accordingly instructed Mr. McCall to receive paper and convert it into specie in the most prudent way he can. I am my dear Sir, with great regard, your afft.
