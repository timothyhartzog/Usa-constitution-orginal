Fails to achieve quorum. Convenes—seven states represented. Elects Cyrus Griffin president. Fails to achieve quorum. Reviews backlog of reports and letters. Receives report on Massachusetts-New York boundary survey. Fails to achieve quorum. Authorizes secretary for foreign affairs to issue sea letters. Sets date for reception of new French minister, comte de Moustier. Elects John Cleves Symmes judge of the North west Territory. Debates appointment of superintendent of In dian affairs for the southern department. Holds audience for comte de Moustier. Receives treasury report on foreign debt. Appoints Samuel Provost and John Rodgers chaplains of Congress, and Richard Winn super intendent of Indian affairs for the southern de partment; debates Kentucky statehood motion.
