Samuel Holten to John Folkersamb Sir. New York 19th March, 1785. I have the pleasure of enclosing a copy of a letter lately received from Capt. Paul Jones, by which you will perceive the state of the busi ness, at the time the letter was dated, respecting the prizes, carried in to Europe by the Bon-homme Richard, & Alliance. 1 I conclude you served on board one of these Vessels, & it gives me pleasure, that you & others in like circumstances are likely to get your pay, ‘tho’ it may be some time first. I am, with real respect, your most obedient (PS.) You will please to excuse my address, as I can’t at this time recol lect your name. FC (MSaE: Holten Papers).
