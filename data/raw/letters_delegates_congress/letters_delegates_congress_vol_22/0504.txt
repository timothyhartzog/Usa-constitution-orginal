He hath been here about a fort night and sails today in the packet. I have been much concern’d for an accident which happen’d to him here. While with us one evening his room was forc’d open & his trunk with papers & money taken out. He recovd. every thing except abt. 40 guineas. As my colleagues write Mr. Short I must beg his excuse for the present. I hope he is well. I am with great respect & esteem yr. affec. friend & servt., Jas.
