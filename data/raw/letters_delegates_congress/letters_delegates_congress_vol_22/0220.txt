I could wish to enlarge on several Subjects but the fear of giving of fence by obtruding my indigested Ideas will induce me to conclude this Letter by requesting your Excellency to assure the Hon. Genl. As sembly of my constant attention to their interest & to those of the U. States—& to be persuaded of the great esteeme with which I have the Honor to be, your Excellencys most obedient & very hble Servt. David Howell P.S. I must request that no copies of my Letters be suffered to be taken for certain reasons.
