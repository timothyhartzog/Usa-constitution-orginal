The committee had been renewed on April 29, and submitted a re port on May 13 that was adopted on the 27th authorizing Malcom, Mercier, and Roo sevelt to determine what damages the memorialists were due because of the inability of the superintendent of finance to make stipulated payments on contracts they had en tered into for supplying the Continental Army in 1782. A second memorial from the contractors of June 13, concerning rations contracts for the garrison at West Point, was also submitted to the three commissioners on June 27.
