James Monroe to James Madison Dear Sir N. York Feby. 1. 1785. I have lately heard nothing from you nor indeed from Richmond. I shod, suspect it arose from the adjournment of the Assembly, if I did not presume, had that event taken place, I shd. been instructed to whose care I might address my letters for you in Fredericksburg or Richmond. My letters to Mr Jones 1 have advisd you of the principles upon wh. our delegation aetd in the questions respecting the places of temporary & permanent residence for Congress; more explicitly per haps than our communications to the Executive have done, altho’ in deed we have been sufficiently explicit to the Executive. 2 The comrs.
