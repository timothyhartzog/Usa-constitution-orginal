John Jay to William Heath Sir, Philadelphia 24th June 1779 I have the pleasure of transmitting to You enclosed an Extract from the Minutes of Congress of the 22nd Inst, by which You will perceive that You are elected to the place of a Commissioner to the Board of War. 1 I have the Honor to be Sir, With great Respect, Your most Obedt. & Hble Servt. LB (DNA: PCC, item 14). 1 In his July 1 response to this letter, Heath reported that because he was “altogether ignorant of the intended duties of the commissioner to the Board of War,” he wished to know “what the general duty of the commissioner is to be, where he is to reside, and whether or not, in case of my acceptance of the trust, I retain my rank in the Army.” When Jay replied that he would be able to retain his Continental rank but would “be paid only as a Commissioner,” Heath declined the appointment.
