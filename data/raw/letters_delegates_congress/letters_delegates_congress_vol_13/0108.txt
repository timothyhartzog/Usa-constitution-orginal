John Jay to George Clinton Sir, Philadelphia 16th June 1779 Copies of two Acts of Congress are herewith enclosed—One of the 14th Inst, recommending to the States to exempt drivers of Waggons, while in the Service from Militia duty—The other of the 15th [i.e. 16th] Inst, on the Subject of your Excellencys Letter of the 7th Inst. 1 I have the honor to be, with great Respect & Esteem, Your Excellency’s, most Obedient Servant. John Jay to the Committee on Vermont Gentlemen, Philadelphia 16th June 1779 You will receive, herewith enclosed, a Copy of an Act of Congress of the 16th Inst, together with Copies of two Letters of the 29th May last, & 7th Inst, from Governor Clinton, and of One from Samuel Minot of the 26th May last to Governor Clinton. 1 The Southern Express is not yet arrived and we have no other Intelligence from Charles town than what consists in a variety of Reports of a victory obtained there over the Enemy. I have the honor to be with great Respect, gentlemen your most Obe. Sert.
