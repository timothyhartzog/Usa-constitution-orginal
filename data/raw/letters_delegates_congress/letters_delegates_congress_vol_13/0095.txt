James Duane to George Clinton Dear Sir, Philad. 14” June 1779. Your Excellency’s Letter to Congress respecting the Revolt has made a serious Impression. 1 I applaud the wisdom & Spirit which it manifests. Congress seem to have relied rather too far on the Modera tion and Passivity of our State: and yet there are Circumstances which Justify their proceeding with Caution.
