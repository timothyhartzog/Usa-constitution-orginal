Should You send the Morris to St Domingo we sub mit the Consignation of the Cargo &ca there to your Direction. We are Sir, Your Most Humble Servt, Frans. Lewis by Order [P.S.] Capt. Calvert has informed the Committee that he had put into your Hands for public use Three Hundred Pieces of 8/8 which he says he was to receive here, and desires us to inform You that Sum is not paid him. Your ut supra, F Lewis (Copy) The 2d of this reed, was signed by F Lewis, James Searle. 3d Novemr. 1779. This will be handed you by Mr. Jos. Conand to whom we have paid your Bill on the Committee dated 10th April in his favor for Fifteen Hundred Spanish Dollars. An Embargo still exists on all Provisions in order to provide sufficient for our Army as well as for the Count D’Estaing’s fleet & Army who are daily expected on this part of the Coast from Georgia where he has lately made a Descent.
