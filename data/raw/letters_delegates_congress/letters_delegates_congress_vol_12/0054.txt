20 February 4, 1779 which I delivered with one from Governor Houstoun of the 2d to Con gress. 1 Inclosed Your Excellency will find an Act of Congress of the 2d Instant for affording succour to South Carolina and Georgia. I shall exert myself for carrying into execution every Article without delay. I am now told the 125 Chests of Arms at Baltimore are dwindled into 125 Arms, “the man ner in which business is transacted here.” 2 Colonel de Cambray has discovered a laudable activity, had he not been detained for his Despatches, he would have begun his journey Yes terday, they are not yet ready, he hopes to get away early tomorrow. Under the Article for sending one or more Engineers, I expect to obtain Colonel Lemoy, 3 who I am told is very skilful and well acquainted with Carolina. I mean to proceed homeward some day in the present month and hope to have the honor of assuring Your Excellency in Person about the middle of the next, 4 that I am with the highest regard and esteem. Sir, Your Most Obedient & Most Humble Servant, H.L.
