Thomas Adams to the Speaker of the Virginia House of Delegates Sir Philadelphia April 23d. 1779 My ill Health and the present deranged State of my Affairs at Home render it indispensibly necessary for me to leave this Place, I have there fore to entreat that you will make my Resignation of my Seat in Congress acceptable to your Honorable House, 1 & to assure them that I have a proper Sense of the high Honor they did me in conferring on me so important a Trust. I have the Honor to be with the greatest Respect, Yr. mo. obt. Sert. Thos. Adams RC (PHi: Gratz Collection). 1 Adams’ letter of resignation was laid before the House of Delegates on May 7. For the controversy created in the house over the resignations from Congress of Adams, Thomas Nelson, Francis Lightfoot Lee, and Richard Henry Lee, see Richard Henry Lee to the Virginia House of Delegates, May 4, 1779, note.
