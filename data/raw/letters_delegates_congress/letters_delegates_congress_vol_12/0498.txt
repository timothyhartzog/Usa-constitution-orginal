William Irvine of the Seventh Pennsylvania Regiment received the promotion as a result of Pennsylvania’s complaint that considering the number of Pennsylvanians in Conti nental service the state did not enjoy its proper share of general officers. As a result of the inquiry conducted into the state’s January 20 memorial on the subject, Congress resolved on May 11 that Pennsylvania was indeed entitled to an additional brigadier, and Irvine was appointed the following day. John Jay to George Washington Dear Sir Philadelphia 12 May 1779 Mrs. De Lancey 1 will be the Bearer of this Letter. When Philadelphia was evacuated, she went round with the Fleet to New York; and left it last October, so circumstanced, as to bring out but very little of her wearing apparel. She is anxious to obtain Permission to send for the Remainder. The Request appears to me reasonable, and therefore I take the Liberty of recommending it to your Excellency’s Attention.
