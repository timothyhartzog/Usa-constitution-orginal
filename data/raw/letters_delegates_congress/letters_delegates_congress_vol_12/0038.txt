John Jay to George Washington Sir, Philadelphia 1st Feby. 1779 Congress have this day passed an Act, of which the enclosed is a copy, on the Subject of your Excellency’s Letter of the 30th Ult., whereby they have resolved that You give such directions, as you may think proper, for arranging the Commissary’s and Quarter Master’s Departments to the Westward &c.' I have the honor to be Sir, with the greatest Respect, Your Excellency’s most Obedt. & very humble Servant, John Jay, Presidt RC (DLC: Washington Papers). In the hand of Henry Brockhoist Livingston and signed by Jay- 'JCC, 13:130-31. Washington’s January 30 letter to Jay, reporting Gen. Thomas McKean to the Public For the Pennsylvania Packet. Water street, Philad. Feb. 1, 1779.
