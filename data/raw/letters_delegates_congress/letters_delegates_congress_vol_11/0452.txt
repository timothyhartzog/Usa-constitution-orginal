'For Philip Schuyler’s resignation, see John Jay to Schuyler, January 15, 1779, note 1. William Floyd to George Clinton Sir, Philadelphia, Jan. 5th. 1779. I do myself the pleasure to enclose this day’s paper to you, con taining the ultimatum of Congress on the affair of Finance, and a publication from Common Sense with what other little news we have here. The publications of Mr. Dean, and Mr. Paine, makes a great talk in this place, and I am afraid will throw Congress and the people at large into violent parties. I am told that the late President declined his seat as president because Congress would not take up Mr. Dean’s publication in a way which he thought proper. In ballotting for a new President, they came in favor of Mr. Jay, who is now our President. There is nothing yet done with respect to the Vermont business,
