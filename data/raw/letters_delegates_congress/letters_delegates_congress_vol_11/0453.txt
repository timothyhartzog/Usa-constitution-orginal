Henry Laurens to John Sullivan Dear Sir, Philadelphia 5th [any 1779 Although I have but a few minutes allowed me for writing by this Messenger Brown I cannot suffer him to pass without acknowledging & thanking you for your favor of the 15th Ulto. 1 Yesterday I received your public address under the Ulto. inclosing the proceedings of a Court Martial in the cases of Crossing & his coadjutors. 2 These I deliver’d to the present President 8c they were read in Congress. I had the honor of resigning the Chair on the 9th December for reasons which then appeared to me to be good 8c which subsequent circum stances have proved to be so. Love of order 8c an high respect for a great Representative, restrains my pen from an explanation at present. 1 am reduced to a private station but do not mean to turn my back on Congress immediately.
