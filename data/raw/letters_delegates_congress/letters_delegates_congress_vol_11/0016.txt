October 2 October 3 October 5 October 6 October 8 October 12 October 13 October 14 October 15 October 16 October 17 Chronology of Congress October 1, 1778-January 31, 1779 Extends embargo to January 31, 1779; requests states to seize provisions to prevent engrossing and speculation. Informs Casimir Pulaski “that it is the duty of every military officer in the service of these states, to yield obedience’’ to the laws of the states. Conducts examination of William Carmichael on the activities of Silas Deane in France. Invites Dr. Richard Price to become a citizen and move to the United States to assist “in regulating their finances.” Lifts limitations on the price of silver and gold. Adopts resolves to suppress “theatrical entertain ments, horse racing, gaming, and such other diver sions as are productive of idleness, [and] dissipa tion.” Orders Washington to take measures for frontier defense. Receives documents from Silas Deane and sched ules continuation of inquiry into charges made against him. Receives intelligence of the distribution of a “Manifesto and Proclamation” from the British peace commissioners. Orders seizure of persons attempting to distribute “manifestoes” of the British commissioners; orders removal of the Convention Army to Charlottes ville, Va. Commends comte d’Estaing for his attempts to as sist the forces of the United States. xii
