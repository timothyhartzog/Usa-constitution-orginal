Jonathan Bayard Smith to George Bryan Dr. Sir York Town Deer. 19 1777 Congress yesterday reed. acot. of the arrival of a Ship from France in 75 days with 48 Cannon, 19 mortars 9 Inch, 61,000 wt. of Brim stone, near 4000 small arms, some powder &c. We have no papers by this Vessell. Genl. Gates has answered some queries proposed by Congress respeetg. Burgoynes conduct in the Convention. He says the military chest & standards were left in Canada. He accounts for the arms unfit for service in the return & the want of scabbards for bayonets 8c of Cartouch boxes &c. 8c concludes that things have been conducted tolerably well.
