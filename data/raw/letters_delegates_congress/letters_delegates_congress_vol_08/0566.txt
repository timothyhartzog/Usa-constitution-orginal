530 January 5, 1778 that another of the Enemies Vessels had run ashore on the Jersey side & was taken by the Jersey People Contents unknown, a most season able relief to our Army who were much in want of those necessary Articles; which a kind Providence has thrown into our hands. Mr Davenport, Mr Colts Clerk has been waiting here some days for directions in his department. As Congress had Committed the affairs of that Department into your hands they neither had time nor Inclination to attend to them. Mr Buchanon will write him what he thinks proper on the subject & he must apply to you for any further direction he wants. 5 Am with the greatest respect your honor’s most Obedt Humble Servt Elipht Dyer [PA.] I had forgot to mention, that previous to the above, some time agoe Congress passed a Resolve that Genii Burgoyne was not to Embark till he paid off in Silver or Gold all the Expenses for his support & his Troops since the Convention. 6 The want of Provisions especially the meat kind for our Army, will we fear, not only prevent calling in such reinforcements as are neces sary to drive How out of Philad (which was fully proposed & there on [are] men Enough ready to step forth on such an occasion from the Jerseys & Elsewhere) but fear will oblige the army we have to scatter or disband, we are really alarmed that that will be the Case.
