110 October 12, 1777 The purpose of this information is that you may indubitably know I gave Congress all the intelligence and opinions concerning you here set down; and then to ask in direct terms if you meant the disrespect ful expressions in your late letter to Congress on the subject of your conduct at Brandywine, to be applied to me? If you did sir, I must inform you, you are mistaken in the matter contained in those ex pressions. My demeanour was entirely devoid of parade and ostenta tion and entirely simple and attentive. I did not gallop my horse at all but when I attempted to rally some of your flying troops. 1 The manner of those expressions, which I suppose you meant for wit and sarcasm, is as unbecoming the soldier as the gentleman, and inconsistent with that plain and dignified simplicity which ought to be the stile of persons in either rank.
