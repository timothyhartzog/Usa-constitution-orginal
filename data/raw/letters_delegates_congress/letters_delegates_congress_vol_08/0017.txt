Chronology of Congress September 19, 1777-January 31, 1778 September 19-26 September 27 September 30 October 1 October 2 October 4 October 7 October 8 October 9-14 October 15 October 17 October 20 October 22 October 23-30 October 29 October 31 November 1 November 4 Delegates in flight to Lancaster, Pa. Convenes at Lancaster; adjourns to York. Convenes at York. Resolves to meet twice daily. Authorizes delegates to draw provisions from Continental commissaries. Commends sundry officers for bravery in defense against General Burgoyne’s northern invasion. Debates “mode of voting” under draft Articles of Confederation. Adopts penalties for “communicating” with the enemy; commends Washington for the “brave exertions” of his army at Germantown. Debates taxation proposals under draft Articles of Confederation. Debates powers of Congress under draft Articles of Confederation. Reorganizes the Board of War. Exonerates Gen. John Sullivan for failure of Staten Island expedition; learns informally of General Gates’ capture of General Burgoyne’s army at Saratoga. Orders inquiry into the conduct of Indian Com missioner George Morgan. Debates and revises draft Articles of Confedera tion. President Hancock takes leave of Congress. Receives official notification of the Saratoga Convention. Elects Henry Laurens president of Congress. Commends General Gates and his army for their defense against Burgoyne’s invasion and various other officers and units for their defense of the Delaware. xiii
