Henry Laurens to Meshech Weare Honorable Sir, Philadelphia. 8th August 1778 Since my last of the 12th July I have been honourd with your fa vors of the 4th & 17th of that Month & am much obliged by the par ticular recitals of such Letters as Your Honor had received from me. 1 Within this Inclosure be pleased to receive Sir an Act of Congress of yesterday Resolving that the expence attending the late abortive expedition against Rhode Island shall be a Public charge. 2 I have the honor to be, With great regard & respect, Honorable Sir, Your most obedient, humble servant, Henry Laurens, President of Congress.
