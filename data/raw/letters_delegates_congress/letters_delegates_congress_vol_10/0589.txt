George Plater to Thomas Johnson Sir, Philadelphia Septr. 3d. 1778. 1 have the Honor herewith to forward to your Excellency the Bills of Exchange, drawn, I hope, in the Manner you directed, & indorsed by Mr. Robert Morris, which I thought best. The Commissions shall be sent as soon as a new Form is made out, which is now under the Consideration of the marine Committee. I have the Honor to be, Yr Excellency’s most obt., & very hble Servt. Geo. Plater P.S. Mr. Morris desires (& we promised it shall be done) that you pay Mr. Stephen Steward eight thousand Pounds Cur & we will endeavor to get the other four thousand from Congress, which is the Amount of the Bills. RC (MdAA).
