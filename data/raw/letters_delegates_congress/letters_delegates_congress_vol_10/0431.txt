Henry Laurens to Benedict Arnold 7th August [1778] Congress are much interrupted in the course of business in their Sessions at the State House by the beating of Drums & noise of the Soldiery at the Guard House. Will you be so obliging as to give the necessary order for preventing in future the inconvenience com plained of. I have the honor to be with great Esteem & Regard &ca. LB (DNA: PCC, item 13). Since my last of the 15th Ulto. I have received your obliging fa vors of the 2nd and 6th of that Month. 1 I am well satisfied with your determination respecting Monsr. Jaussaud; I have a long Letter from Mr. Delagay£ but tis French, and I have not yet read it.
