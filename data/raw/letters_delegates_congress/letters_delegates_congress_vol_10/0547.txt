Henry Laurens to William Heath Sir, Philadelphia 28th Augt. 1778 I have not had occasion to trouble you since the 8th Inst, nor have I since received any addition to your former favours. The present is intended only as a Cover to an Act of Congress of the 26th for settling Rations when it shall be necessary by commutation of species, 1 and to assure you that I remain with great Esteem & Re gard, Sir, Your obedient & most humble servant, Henry Laurens, President of Congress.
