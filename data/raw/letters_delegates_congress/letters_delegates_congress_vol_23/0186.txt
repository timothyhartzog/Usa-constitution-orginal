The non attendance of some States not only weakens the government by rendering it dilatory and inefficient in it’s administration—but throws an unequal expence on those States which do attend. The policy of our State is extensive, manly & liberal and her admin istration Just, wise and excellent compared with many of the other States particularly this. How far the States have adopted the Continental impost System, the proposed alteration of the 8th Art. of the Confederation, and have vested Congress with commercial powers, you will see by official pa pers transmitted to our Legislature. 2 They must all I think if properly pursued be finally adopted.
