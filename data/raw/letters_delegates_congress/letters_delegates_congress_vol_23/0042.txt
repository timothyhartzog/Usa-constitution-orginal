Charles Thomson to the States Sir, (Circular) Office of Secretary of Congress, Novr 7. 1785. I have the honor to transmit to your Excellency herewith enclosed two copies of the continuation of the journal of Congress from 29 Septr. to the close of the Session, one copy for the legislative & the other for the executive branch of Government. In conformity to the Act of the 17 August I enclose also a state of the representation from the beginning of Oct to the 1 Monday in Novr. & have to request the favor of Yr Exy. to lay it before your legislature. 1 With the greatest respect, I have the honor to be, Your Exy’s Most Obedt and Most humble Servt.
