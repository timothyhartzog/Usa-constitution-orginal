Rufus King to John Hancock Dear Sir, New York 30 March 1786 Mr. Ramsay the chairman of congress told me a few days since that he had written to you informing that he should soon leave congress, & that he wished to know whether your Health would allow you to come on. This letter was not written in consequence of any order or direction of congress, but can be considered only as a volunteer Affair in the writer.
