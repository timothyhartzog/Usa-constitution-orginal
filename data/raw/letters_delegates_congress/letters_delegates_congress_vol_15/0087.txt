April 19, 1780 53 Mr. Livingston is gone into Jersey. 3 I am one of a Committee directed to repair to the army, for the purpose of arranging all the staff departments, thereof; this business and that of visiting the dif ferent posts East of Jersey will take much of my time, so that General Scott will be left alone, and altho our business will not Suffer In his hands, Yet Sickness may prevent his constant attendance; would It not, therefore, be prudent to hasten Mr. Duane on. I hope Your Excellency has received the act of Congress allowing pay & subsist- ance to the men to be raised for the defence of our frontiers. 4 Please to make my respects acceptable to Mrs. Clinton. I am, D’r Sir, your Excellency’s most obedient Servant Ph: Schuyler. RC (N: Clinton Papers); Clinton, Papers (Hastings), 5:642-45.
