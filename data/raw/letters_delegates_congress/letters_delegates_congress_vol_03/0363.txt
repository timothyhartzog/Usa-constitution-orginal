March 4, 1776 329 & we hear from good Authority that 5 tons of Powder has arrived safe at N Carolina. This is all the News I have heard since I have been in Town. As to Politics—you know the Letters of Congress People should be silent on that subject in these Times, when Letters often miscarry &c. God bless you & give you Health. I am &c.
