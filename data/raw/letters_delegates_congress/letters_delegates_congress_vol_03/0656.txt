Samuel Huntington to Nathaniel Shaw, Jr. Sir Philadelphia 4th May 1776 I am favoured with yours of the 24th April, note the contents, but before it came to hand, I had the pleasure of procuring you to be ap pointed Agent, being the most Suitable person I could think of. The Resolution of Congress appointing you will doubtless come to hand before this reaches you. 1 We have obtaind an order of Congress to ex change your powder in this City for continental powder in New London, the Committee will Soon give you particular directions on that Subject.
