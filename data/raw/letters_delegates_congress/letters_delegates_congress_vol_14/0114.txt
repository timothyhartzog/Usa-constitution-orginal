80 October 15, 1779 John Jay to Samuel Huntington Sir Philadelphia 15 Octr. 1779 Governor Livingston having consented that his Son whom Con gress have honored with the Commission of Lt. Colonel, should go with me to Spain, I join with him in requesting the Favor of Con gress to grant him their Permission. 1 1 have the Honor to be with great Respect & Esteem, Your Excel- lencys most obedt Servt. President Huntington’s October 15 letter to Livingston enclosing this resolve is in PCC, item 14, fol. 208. 2 This is the last Jay letter printed in this edition of delegate correspondence. To Governor Clinton, he recommended that New York more vigorously press its claims to Indian lands, and to Livingston he sent a cipher, which is discussed at length by the editors of Jay’s papers, to facilitate their correspondence while he was abroad.
