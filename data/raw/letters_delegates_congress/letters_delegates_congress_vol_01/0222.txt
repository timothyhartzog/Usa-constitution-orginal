James Duane’s Notes of Debates Wednesday 12th Oct 1774 Mr Ward. On Trade. 1st, Every man is born free and may chuse his own form of Gov ernment. 2d, Bound by the compact, by charter between Crown and people. Here no Acts of Parliament can bind. 3d, Colonies obliged by this, acts only by compulsion. 4th, Principle of Protection gives them a right to a compensation, but state the amount and we have overpaid them. 5th, Necessity of the case does not apply, the Colo nies distinct command under one head and [under?] 1 one kingdom should not be governed by another. 6th, One Company would not leave it to another to settle the terms. 7th, As to the prudence of the measure,—the Nation and Parliament were corrupt—would any chuse a factor of this character? 8th, Oppressive measures against East Indies. 9th, Giving up this point is yielding all. J. Adams. Approves it—my amendment. Mr Lee. 1st, Ministry may say they 2d, Slitting mills avoided by this Motion offer a proposition. 2 External Lynch.
