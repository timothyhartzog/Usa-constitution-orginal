1774 September 5 September 17 September 27 September 28 September 30 October 1 October 14 October 18 October 21 October 22 October 26 1775 May 10 May 17 Chronology of Congress September 1774 to August 1775 Congress convenes at Carpenters’ Hall; elects Peyton Randolph president, Charles Thomson secretary. Endorses Suffolk Resolves from Massachusetts. Adopts nonimportation agreement, to begin Decem ber 1. Orders Joseph Galloway’s plan of union to lie on the table. Resolves to halt exports to Great Britain, Ireland, and the West Indies effective September 10, 1775. Resolves to prepare an address to the king. Adopts declaration of grievances and rights. Approves the Association. Approves an address to the people of Great Britain and one to the inhabitants of the colonies. Agrees to reconvene on May 10, 1775, “unless the redress of grievances, which we have desired, be obtained before that time.” Elects Henry Middleton president. Approves an address to the king and a letter to Quebec. Congress dissolves itself. Second Continental Congress convenes at Penn sylvania State House; reelects President Peyton Randolph and Secretary Charles Thomson. Resolves to ban exports to British colonies failing to join the Association. xxiv
