August 16 August 19 August 20 August 23 August 26 August 27 August 30 September 3 September 6 September 9 September 11 September 16 September 17 September 20 September 22 September 25 Chronology of Congress August-December 1776 Censures Commodore Esek Hopkins. Orders Commodore Hopkins to resume command of Continental fleet; adopts extensive new instructions for Indian commissioners in middle department. Reads draft Articles of Confederation and orders them printed in preparation for debate in committee of the whole. Authorizes additional troops on Continental estab lishment for frontier defense. Adopts measures for relief of disabled soldiers and seamen. Resolves to encourage foreign mercenaries to desert from British army. Adopts plan to improve postal system. Receives Gen. John Sullivan’s written report on Lord Howe’s proposal for peace conference. Designates Benjamin Franklin, John Adams, and Edward Rutledge to meet with Lord Howe. Revises style of Continental commissions, replacing “United Colonies” with “United States.” Committee meets with Lord Howe on Staten Island. Adopts new plan for a Continental Army of 88 battalions and system of bounties for recruitment of officers and soldiers. Adopts Plan of Treaties; receives report of the com mittee appointed to confer with Lord Howe and orders it published. Adopts Articles of War. Sends committee to New York “to enquire into the state of the army.” Resolves to send committee to Ticonderoga to im prove administration of northern army. xiii
