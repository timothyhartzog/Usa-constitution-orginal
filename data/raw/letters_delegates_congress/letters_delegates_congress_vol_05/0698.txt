Benjamin Harrison to the Maryland Council of Safety Sir Baltimore Deer. 25th. 1776 Congress are in immediate Want of a fast sailing Vessell. Every Endeavor has been used to procure one without Success nor have they the least Chance left of getting one unless your honorable Board will spare Capt. Martin’s Brig. I should by no Means ask the Favor, knowing how necessary this Vessel is to the State of Maryland, if the Occasion was not of the most pressing Nature. Should the Board be so obliging as to part with her, Congress ex pect to pay what the Vessel & Cargo cost with every Charge of Wages & Outfit. You’l please to lay this matter before the Board and favor me with their Answer by the Express. 1 I have the Honor to be, your obedt. Servant, Benja.
