Sir, I am directed by Congress to transmit you the enclosed Resolve, and to request your Attention to it. 1 The dangerous Intercourse be tween our Enemies in New York and Staten Island, and the internal Enemies of America in Different Parts of New Jersey, calls for the immediate Interposition of Government to suppress it. You will therefore be pleased, in Conjunction with your Council & Assembly, to take such Measures for this End, as you shall judge most expedient. I have the Honour to be, with the utmost Esteem, Sir, your most obed. & very hble Sert. J. H. Presidt.
