James Wilson to Robert Morris Dear Sir Baltimore 28th Deer. 1776 The Committee of Congress at Philadelphia are directed by a late Resolution of Congress to contract for erecting a Magazine at Carlisle. 1 I shall be at that Place in a short Time; and will give every Assistance in my Power to those whom you employ. You are much wanted in Congress; tho’ I know you are of essential Service at Philadelphia. I am, Dear Sir, Yours sincerely, James Wilson RC (DNA: PCC, item 78). ’For this resolution of December 27, see JCC, 6:1044.
