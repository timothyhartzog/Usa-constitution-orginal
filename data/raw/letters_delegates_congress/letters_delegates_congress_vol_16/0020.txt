xvi Chronology of Congress February 19 February 20 February 22 February 23 February 24 February 27 February 28 to recede from previous instruction insisting on the free navigation of the Mississippi River. Orders inquiry into the causes of the delay in the shipment of clothing and arms from France. Orders the reinforcement and resupply of the southern department; appoints Robert Morris superintendent of finance. Assigns March 1 for completing and ratifying the confederation. Debates and recommits report on the Hartford economic convention. Doubles postage rates; adopts plan for ratifying ceremonies. Commends John Paul Jones for “distinguished bravery and military conduct, . . . particularly. . . over the British ship of war Serapis”-, elects Alexander McDougall secretary of marine. Postpones election of secretary at war to Octo ber 1; imposes restrictions on ornate military uniforms and decorations; refers old business to the new Confederation Congress.
