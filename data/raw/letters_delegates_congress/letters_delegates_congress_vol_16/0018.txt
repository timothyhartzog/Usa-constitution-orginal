xiv Chronology of Congress November 14 November 16 November 17 November 22 November 23 November 24 November 27 November 28 November 30 December 1 December 4 December 6 December 7 December 9 December 11 December 15 December 19 December 21 December 22 December 23 December 27 Authorizes capital punishment for persons supplying the enemy with provisions or military stores. Receives Committee at Headquarters report; confers with Pennsylvania officials on provisions embargo. Resolves to appeal to France for 25 million livres in aid. Adopts appeal to the king of France; appoints William Geddes paymaster general. Rescinds election of William Geddes as paymaster general. Receives report on treasury inquiry. Adopts measures for outfitting Continental ships; adopts additional privateer instructions. Extends half-pay provisions to general officers; instructs Franklin on procuring aid from France and cultivating commerce with Morocco. Adopts revised commissary regulations. Adopts statement endorsing Arthur Lee’s conduct abroad. Prohibits unauthorized military purchases; appoints Simeon De Witt geographer to the Continental Army. Commends Benjamin Tallmadge’s troops for Long Island raid; halts removal of Convention Army from Virginia. Observes day of prayer and thanksgiving. Adopts instructions for consul to France, William Palfrey. Appoints John Laurens “envoy extraordinary” to France. Resolves to appoint a minister to Russia. Appoints Francis Dana minister to Russia. Debates impact of John Laurens’ appointment on Benjamin Franklin’s mission in France; launches study of the conditions of Henry Laurens’ imprisonment. Appeals to the states to fulfill Continental troop quotas. Adopts instructions for special envoy to France, John Laurens. Instructs Benjamin Franklin on John Laurens’ mission to France.
