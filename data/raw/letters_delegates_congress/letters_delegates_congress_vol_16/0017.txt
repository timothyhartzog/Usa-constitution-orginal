Chronology of Congress xiii October 4 October 6 October 10 October 13 October 14 October 16 October 17 October 18 October 21 October 23 October 24 October 25-31 October 30 October 31 November 1 November 3 November 4 November 7 November 9 November 10 November 13 Adopts instructions for John Jay on navigation of the Mississippi River and southwestern boundaries. Elects officers for hospital department. Adopts Virginia proposal to reimburse state expenses related to cession of western lands and to require that ceded lands ‘ ‘be disposed of for the common benefit of the United States.” Appoints Daniel Morgan brigadier general; creates third chamber of accounts. Votes memorial for Baron de Kalb; commends various officers and troops for bravery at the battle of Camden. Receives proceedings of the Hartford conven tion of New England states. Adopts letter of instruction for John Jay. Instructs John Adams on peace negotiations; sets day of prayer and thanksgiving. Endorses proposal to receive Cherokee delegation; revises Continental Army estab lishment. Receives report on the victory at King’s Mountain. Sends urgent appeal to the states on the present distresses of the army. Debates ways and means proposals. Confirms Nathanael Greene’s appointment to command of the southern department. Orders cavalry reinforcement to southern department. Authorizes drawing additional bills of exchange on Benjamin Franklin. Rewards captors of Maj. John Andre. Apportions $6 million specie tax, to be collected chiefly in kind; appoints William Palfrey consul to France. Authorizes prisoner-of-war exchange. Adopts letter of appeal to the states on present emergency. Adopts measures to curtail enemy fraudulent use of American privateer commissions; directs steps for reducing forage expenses. Commends troops engaged in the victory at King’s Mountain.
