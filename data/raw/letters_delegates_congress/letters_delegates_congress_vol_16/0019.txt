Chronology of Congress XV December 29 January 3 January 5 January 6 January 8 January 9 January 10 January 12 January 15 January 17 January 19 January 24 January 31 February 2 February 3 February 5 February 7 February 8 February 12 February 15 Commissions John Adams to negotiate a treaty of amity and commerce with the United Provinces. Appoints committee to confer with Pennsylva nia officials on the mutiny of the Pennsylvania Line. Empowers the mutiny committee “to take such measures as may appear necessary to quiet the disturbances”; threatens retaliation for British mistreatment of American prisoners. Revives committee for the reorganization of the executive departments. Endorses proposal to receive Delaware Indian delegation. Recommends prosecution of former clothier general James Mease for “a high abuse of office.” Authorizes establishment of a permanent office for the department of foreign affairs. Endorses treasury inquiry report acquitting commissioners of the chambers of accounts. Adopts new fiscal appeal to the states from New Hampshire to Pennsylvania. Appoints John Cochran director of the hospital department and John Pierce paymaster general. Opens debate on fiscal crisis. Receives report on the mutiny of the Pennsylvania Line. Receives committee of the whole recommendation for a 5 percent impost. Rejects Pennsylvania appeal for an emergency pay response for the Pennsylvania Line. Recommends state action to empower Congress to levy a 5 percent impost. Commends General Parsons’ troops for their attack at Morrisania; defines alien property rights under the Franco-American treaties. Adopts plan to create departments of finance, war, and marine. Receives news of Gen. Daniel Morgan’s victory at Cowpens, S.C. Receives Maryland act authorizing ratification of the Articles of Confederation.
