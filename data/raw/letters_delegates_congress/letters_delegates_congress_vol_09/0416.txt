Henry Laurens Dear sir, 8 April 1778 After your Packet had been sealed up, I discovered a mistake which this is slipped in to inform you of 8c to save you some trouble. I am in possession of Mr. Holt’s Letter & will by the very first opportunity pay into the hand of Mr. Morris or of his Agent here if he does not come within the course of this Week to York, the intended Money 300 Dollars. You may tell that Gentleman now, that 200 comes from a Brother Printer John Wells Junior who lately had his own House in Charles Town burnt over his head in the dreadful conflagration in that Town, but had the good fortune by accumulated assistance to save his vast collection of Books, his household furniture. Paper, Types 8c almost everything 8c within a Week of the accident proceeded in his Publications. H.
