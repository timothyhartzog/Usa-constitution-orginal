McKean had gone to Lancaster for a meet ing of the Pennsylvania supreme court. “I arrived here on Tuesday morning and was escorted into Town by a number of Gentlemen of the Law, Justices of the peace &c. besides the Officers, who met me near to Mrs. Scott’s. Matters have been conducted equal to my most sanguine hopes, indeed every thing is as I would wish it.” Concerning Charles Lee’s parole and public affairs in Delaware: "General Rodney is appointed President of the Delaware State. General Lee will be here today; tho’ I believe, he is not yet finally exchanged. . . . The In habitants of Delaware are said to be on the verge of a total Revolution to Whiggism, and that the most spirited measures are adopted there.” RC (PHi).
