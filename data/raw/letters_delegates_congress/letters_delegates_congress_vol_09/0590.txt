Henry Laurens to William Thomson Dear Sir, 1st jyj a y 1778, The papers 1 which I send inclosed with this have afforded much satisfaction in this Quarter, I trust the whole Union will be as well pleased as we are. I may add that you may almost depend upon it as a fact, France has confirmed a Treaty with our Commissioners & acknowledge the Sovereignty & Independence of America, & that a few days will bring a confirmation of this grand event. My present circumstances will not allow me to write so fully to my friends as I wish, I work 16 & sometimes 21 Hours in a day which affords short intervals for repose. My best wishes attend you & Mrs Thomson & all yours. I am with great regard &ca.
