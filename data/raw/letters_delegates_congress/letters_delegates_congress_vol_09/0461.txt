In the hand of Henry Laurens. This text has been produced by con flating two physically discrete but organically related manuscripts among Laurens’ papers. The first is seven pages in length and consists of three distinct sections: a brief account of congressional action on half pay between April 17 and 21 (pp. 1-2); a more extended description of Congress’ treatment of this issue on April 21 (pp. 3-6); and Laurens’ own reflections on the subject of half pay (p. 7). The second manuscript is two pages long and contains further observations by Laurens on half pay that are somewhat similar to those in his letter to William Livingston of April 19, 1778.
