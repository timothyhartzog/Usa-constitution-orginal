Delegates pressed strongly to have the Originals delivered to them & the Virginia Delegates & the Congress to keep attested Cop ies, but it was opposed 8c the Letters referred to a Comee. There was no Objection to those Delegates taking attested Copies. Gen. Washn. has sent to Gen. Howe a spirited Letter informing Him that whatever Severities are inflicted on Col. Allen shall be retaliated on Brig. Gen. Prescot 8c the like as to other prisoners, a Copy of the Letter was read in Congress. 2 Another letter was reed, from Washn. recom [mendin] g 2 French Gentn. who offered to supply this Continent with Pow- j der 8c these Gentn. being in Town our Secret Comee. were desired to treat with them. 3 Leave given to Major Preston to go for 2 Weeks to Amboy, after much Opposition. A Guard of 5 men ordered imme diately to convoy the hard Money to Gen. Schuyler 8c to take with them the Men who informed agt. Sir John Johnson. 40 Dollars al lowed for their Expences. A Comee. directed to give proper orders to Gen Schuyler on this occasion. 4 Myself from the Comee. made Re port on Capt. Simeon Sellecks Petition. He commands a small Priva teer in Connectt. & lately took at Turtle Bay in the Sound, Kings Stores to the Amot. of £1500 lawful Money of Connectt. We al lowed Him £100 like Money as a Reward for his Expences, Trouble and Risque, he gave up his Prize for the Continental Use. Debates Whether to stop Lieut. Moncrief who some Months ago had Leave to go to England 8c is now about going. He was at length allowed to go.
