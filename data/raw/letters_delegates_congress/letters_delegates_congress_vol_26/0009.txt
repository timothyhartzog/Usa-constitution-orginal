List of Delegates to Congress This compilation contains a list of the 435 delegates to Congress elected by the states from 1774 to 1789 as well as a comprehensive record of attendance of the 344 who actually served. The list of dele gates has been compiled from contemporary state records, but as there remain a few gaps in the legislative proceedings of some of the states and because delegates could be elected at any time to fill unex pired terms caused by death or resignations, it is not possible to claim with certainty that the list is complete. Dates of attendance have been compiled from a variety of sources, though primarily from the jour nals of Congress and the delegates’ correspondence. These dates, however, are at times inevitably conjectural, and it is simply not possi ble to record every interruption in service caused by illness or brief trips home, especially of delegates living within easy access of Congress. Brief biographical sketches of all the delegates are available in the Biographical Directory of the United States Congress, 1774-1989, and fuller sketches of more than half of the delegates can be found in the Dictionary of American Biography.
