These are the States too which so pertenaciously insist that the revenues to be derived from commerce should be thrown into common stock. Sure I am that if our State parts with its advantage in point of revenue from trade, it must soon suc cumb under its burdens & dwindle into insignificance. I have sent you these papers to be laid before the Hon. the Genl. As sembly at their meeting in May. I pray you to present herewith my most dutiful respects & to assure them of my careful attention to their interests. With perfect consideration, I have the honor to be, Dear Sir, your most obedient & very hble Servt, David Howell P.S.
