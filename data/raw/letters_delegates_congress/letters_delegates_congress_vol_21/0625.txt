Edward Hand to Jasper Yeates Dear Yeates Annapolis 7th May 1784 Inclosed you have The requisitions for the Current year. 1 They are not such as I could wish them. You have also, in manuscript, the hum ble petition of Congress for powers to lay general restrictions on Com merce, 2 both this & the printed paper explain themselves. Congress persist in the resolution of rising the 3d of June—if they do, they must wrangle less, & do more business, or leave much un done. The Season still continues backward here, but I believe it is the healthier for it. I beg my duty, love & Compliments to all with you & am Dear Yeates, very affectionately yours, Edwd. Hand [PS.] Tell Col. A. Hubley that our old Acquaintance Captn. Carbury has returned from England & is in the hands of the Law. He is to be delivered to the Executive of Pennsylvania if they desire it.
