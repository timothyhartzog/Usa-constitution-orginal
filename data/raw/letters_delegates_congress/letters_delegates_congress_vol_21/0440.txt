We replied, no, if these lands are not of fered for sale the ensuing spring, they will be taken from us all by ad venturers. We will not therefore put it out of our power by the execu tion of a deed to sell them ourselves, if Congress will not. A member from Rhode island 2 then moved that Congress should accept. An other from Jersey 3 proposed as an amendment a proviso that it should not amount to an acknowlegement of our right. We told them we were not authorised to admit any conditions or provisoes, that their acceptance must be simple, absolute & unqualified or we could not execute. On the question there were 6 ayes, Jersey no, S. Carolina & Pennsylva. divided. The matter dropped & the house proceeded to other business. About an hour after the dissenting Pennsylvanian 4 asked & obtained leave to change his no, into aye. The vote then passed & we executed the deed. We have desired an exemplification of it under the seal of the states which we shall transmit you by the post if no safer opportunity occurs. This shews the wisdom of the as sembly in not tacking any new conditions, which would certainly have defeated their accomodating intentions.
