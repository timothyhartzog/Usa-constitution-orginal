734 July 20,1784 Indians towd. us, as well as of the soil, waters & in genl, the natural view of the country. Captn. Denny whom we saw at Annapolis accom panies me. I am sensible of the fatigue I shall undergo but am resolv’d to sustain it. I am with my best wishes for yr. health yr. affectionate friend & servant, Jas. Charles Thomson to Benjamin Bankson Philad July 20. 1784 I have reed, your letter of the 12 with the papers enclosed which I have forwarded as agreeably to the intention of the hon Comee. I am glad to find the printer has finished the journal of last session. I hope he will now take up and finish what remains of 1783, and that he will use as much industry as possible in compleating it. MrJany 1 has called on me to settle for binding the Journal. He says he delivered 143 Volumes of 1779 to Mr Bond. Please to examine what memoran dum there is of the delivery & send it to me.
