Elias Boudinot to the States Sir, Princeton 1st Nov. 1783 I am honored by the commands of Congress to transmit to your Ex cellency the enclosed certified copy of their Acts of the 7th and 21st October last, relative to their residence in Maryland and New-Jersey. 1 And am, with great respect &c. E. B. LB (DNA: PCC, item 16). Addressed: “His Excellency, The Governor of Maryland.” 1 Congress had been debating the question of its permanent residence since October 6. Its October 7 decision for a Delaware River site had been amended on October 21 to provide for a second site “near the lower falls of Potomac," which would alternate with the former as a federal capital. A committee had been appointed previously to report on a Delaware River site, and when a similar committee to report on a Potomac site was appointed on October 30, President Boudinot was belatedly directed to communicate the resolutions of the 7th and 21st to the several states. See JCC, 25:654-59, 711-14, 767-70; Pa. Archives, 1st ser. 10:136-39; and Charles Thomson to Hannah Thomson, October 21, 1783, note 1.
