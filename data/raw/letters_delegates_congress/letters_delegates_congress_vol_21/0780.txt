744 July 27, 1784 Charles Thomson to Benjamin Bankson Dear Sir, Philada. 27 July 1784 I reed, yours of the 19 with the 6 copies of the journal for the office of finance. After the 2 doz are sent up for the Superint I shall be obliged if you will send me 7 doz more. I likewise reed, the copies rela tive to Longchamp; but there is another letter or note from the Minis ter which I should be glad to have & which is not among those sent. As the manuscript Journals which I brought with me does not come lower than Jany 1779, and the Originals are necessary to enable me to compleat the secret and foreign affairs journal as directed, I shall be glad provided Mr Remsen comes in the stage if you will pack up in a box as many of the original manuscript Journals from Jany 1779 as he can conveniendy bring & commit them to his charge. I am Sr, Yrs. 1 LB (PHi: Thomson Letterbook). 1 Thomson also noted at the foot of this entry that he had enclosed the following newspapers: “Packet 1730, 1731, Pen. Journal 1715." I had no apprehension of your meeting with any difficulty in receiving the amount of the bills sent as they were drawn on Mr Harwood at short sight.
