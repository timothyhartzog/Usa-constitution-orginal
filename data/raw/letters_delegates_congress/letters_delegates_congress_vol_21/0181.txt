Maryland Delegates to the Maryland Assembly Gentn. Princeton 3d Novr. 1783, at Night. To-day Congress proceeded to the election of a president when Ths. Mifflin Eqr. a delegate from Pennsylvania was chosen. This Gentleman being absent a chairman was elected—when a motion was brought for ward and agreed to, that in the absence of the president the chairman adjourn Congress on the 6th instant to meet at Annapolis on the 26. 1 This step was taken in consequence of some doubts as to the propri ety of the resolutions for adjournment under the late Congress. We flatter ourselves that the business is now closed; and beg leave to sub mit it to the wisdom of the house the adoption of such arrangements and measures previous to the 26—as may best serve to accommodate Congress—and to render their residence, and the residence of for eign ministers agreeable. With the greatest respect we have the honor to be, Gentlemen, your most ob st. Dani. Carroll James McHenry RC (MdBJ-G: Miscellaneous Manuscripts). Written by McHenry and signed by McHenry and Carroll. 1 After the presentation of the delegates’ credentials this day, the opening day of the third Congress under the Articles of Confederation, Congress elected Thomas Mifflin president and, in his absence, Daniel Carroll as chairman. On a motion of James Duane, Congress extended their thanks to the departing president, Elias Boudinot, “in testimony of their approbation of his conduct in the chair, and in the execution of the public business.” See JCC, 25:799. According to John Francis Mercer, “Our president was cheered, members of the Congress rising as he left the Chair"—perhaps the only president accorded such an honor. Mercer to Edmund Randolph, November 3, 1783, Tr (from original in the possession of Roy G. Fitzgerald, 1950), Herbert E. Klingelhofer Collection, Bethesda, Md. For James McHenry’s motion later this day to empower and direct Chairman Carroll “to adjourn Congress on the 6th instant, to meet at Annapolis on the 26th, agreeable to the resolution of the 21 of October last,” see JCC, 25:802.
