iv Editorial Method and Apparatus TEXTUAL DEVICES The following devices will be used in this work to clarify the text. [roman] [italic] (italic) One or two words missing and not conjecturable. More than two words missing; subjoined footnote estimates amount of material missing. Number or part of a number missing or illegible. Blank space in manuscript; explanation in sub joined footnote. Conjectural reading for missing or illegible matter; question mark inserted if reading is doubtful. Editorial insertion in the text. Matter crossed out in manuscript but restored. DESCRIPTIVE SYMBOLS The following symbols are used in this work to describe the kinds of documents drawn upon. When more than one symbol is used in the de scriptive note, the first to appear is that from which the main text is taken.
