Index In this index descriptive subentries are arranged chronologically and in ascending order of the initial page reference. They may be preceded, however, by the subentry “identified and by document subentries arranged alphabetically—diary entries, letters, notes, resolu tions, and speeches. An ornament (■&) separates the subentry “identi fied” and document subentries from descriptive subentries. Inclusive page references are supplied for descriptive subentries; for a docu ment, only the page on which it begins is given. Eighteenth-century printed works are indexed both by author and by short title. Other printed works are indexed when they have been cited to document a substantive point discussed in the notes, but not when cited merely as the location of a document mentioned.
