John Hancock to Richard Caswell Sir, Philada. August 22d. 1777. The Congress having received Intelligence from Virginia that the Fleet of the Enemy were seen near the Capes of Chesapeak, on the 15th inst and not knowing what may be their Views upon the Southern Coast, have come to the enclosed Resolve, earnestly recommending to you to have all the naval and military Stores removed, with the utmost Expedition, to Places of greater Security. 1 I have the Pleasure to congratulate you on the Success of Genl. Stark, who commanded a Party of two thousand mostly Militia, in the Northern Department. For the Particulars I beg Leave to refer you to the enclosed printed Account. 2 I have the Honour to be, with the greatest Respect, Sir, your most hble Servt. John Hancock Presidt RC (OCIWHi).
