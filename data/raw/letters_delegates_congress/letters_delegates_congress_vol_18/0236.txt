Thomas Edison, formerly a clerk in Secre tary Charles Thomson’s office, had allowed himself to be drawn into the scheme by Beckwith but betrayed his confederates at a critical moment. As a result, two of the loyalist plotters, Laurence Maar and John Moody, were captured and executed, although a third, Moody’s brother James, escaped and lived to tell the tale. In his “Narrative,” Edison is identified as “one Addison.” John Hanson to Louis Le Begue Duportail Sir, Philadelphia, 17th. Nov. 1781 I have the honor of enclosing you a copy of an Act of Congress of the 16th instant, by which you will observe that you are promoted to the rank of a Major General in the Armies of the United States. 1 This Act is so very explicit on the subject of your distinguished Merit, as to foreclose the Sentiments my feelings would prompt me to express. I will therefore only beg leave to congratulate you on the occasion, and to assure you that you have my best wishes for future prosperity and felicity. I have the honor to be &c. &c.
