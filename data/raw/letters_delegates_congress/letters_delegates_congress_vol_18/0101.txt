See JCC, 21:944, 969; and PCC, item 64, fols. 516-19. Thomas McKean to Michael Hillegas and Joseph Nourse Sir, Philada Septr. 21st. 1781. Inclosed herewith I have the pleasure to transmit you two Acts of Congress, one of the 11th, the other of the 19th instant, whereby you will perceive you are again elected Treasurer of the United States of America. 1 You were chosen by an unanimous ballot, which is the fullest approbation of your past conduct. I wish you satisfac tion in the appointment & all manner of happiness, and am, Sir, with respect &c &c, T. M K. President LB (DNA: PCC, item 16). Addressed: “To Michael Hillegas Esquire. N.B. A similar letter to Mr Joseph Nourse Register of the Treasury of the U.S. mutatis mutandis.” 1 The act “of the 11th” was undoubtedly the new “Ordinance for Regulating the Treasury, and Adjusting the Public Accounts.” That of the 19th recorded the election of Hillegas and Nourse to the positions of treasurer and register, respectively, under the new ordinance.
