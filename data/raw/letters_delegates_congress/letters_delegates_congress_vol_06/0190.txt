158 January 29, 1777 We have no News here. The Events which take place in the Jerseys must be known in Boston before you can be informd of them from this Place. There is a Report that a Party of the Jersey Militia fell in with a larger Party of the Enemy, killed about twenty and took a greater Num ber Prisoners besides fifty three Waggons and Provisions. This is believd. It is also said that General Heath has taken Fort Washington. If it be so, we shall soon have the News confirmd. 3 RG (NN). 1 MS damaged, approximately ten words missing. 3 MS damaged, at least one line missing. 3 Remainder of MS missing. John Hancock to the Executive Committee Sir, Baltimore Jany 29th 1777. You will perceive from the Resolves herewith transmitted, that Con gress do not at present see the Necessity of moving the public Stores from Philada., and that if any Circumstances should render such a Measure proper, you are to take such Steps therein as you think will contribute most to the public Good. 1 I beg Leave to request your Attention to the other Resolves, and have the Honour to be, Sir, Your most obed. & very hble Servt. John Hancock Presidt [P.5.] Pray did you Send an Express to Boston with the Letters Inclos’d you in mine of Jany. 10th?
