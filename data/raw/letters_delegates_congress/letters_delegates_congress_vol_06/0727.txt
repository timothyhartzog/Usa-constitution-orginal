INDEX In this index descriptive subentries are arranged chronologically and in ascending order of the initial page reference. They may be preceded, however, by the subentry “identified” and by document subentries ar ranged alphabetically—diary entries, letters, notes, resolutions, and speeches. An ornament separates the subentry “identified” and docu ment subentries from descriptive subentries. Inclusive page references are supplied for descriptive subentries; for a document, only the page on which it begins is given. Eighteenth-century printed works are indexed both by author and by short title. Other printed works are indexed when they have been cited to document a substantive point discussed in the notes, but not when cited merely as the location of a document men tioned. Delegates who attend Congress during the period covered by this volume appear in boldface type.
