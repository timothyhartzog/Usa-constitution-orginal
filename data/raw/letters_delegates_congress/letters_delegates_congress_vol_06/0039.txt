I lament your hard fortune, and unless some measures are immediately taken to prevent the infamous practices of the Privateers, America will soon be in a state of general confusion—one Part warring against another, and the defence less Southern colonies become a devoted prey to their more formidable Eastern neighbours. I laid your letter relative the Capture of the Brig Joseph before the congress without any embellishment or reserve. The New England Gentlemen, felt the force of the Imputation but considered it as too general. I have an order that our President should write the President and Council of Mass’tts requesting to make an immediate en quiry after the Vessell and the Pirates who have taken her, secure her and them & report immediately to congress.
