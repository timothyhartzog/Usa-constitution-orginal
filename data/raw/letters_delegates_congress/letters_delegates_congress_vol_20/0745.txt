September 26, 1783 711 We have the Honour to be, with the greatest Respect, your very obed Servants, John Montgomery Richard Peters RC (PHi: Gratz Collection). Written by Peters and signed by Peters and Montgomery. Addressed: “Honble Speaker of the Assembly of Pennsilvania.” 1 The Pennsylvania Assembly was seeking congressional approval to negotiate with the Indians for the purchase of lands the state wished to grant to Pennsylvania veterans. The assembly’s instructions “relative to the proposed Purchase” had been referred on September 13 to a committee consisting of James Duane, Daniel Car roll, Benjamin Hawkins, Arthur Lee, and Richard Peters, which already had under consideration a number of issues concerning Continental Indian policy. The committee’s report on these instructions, which was written by Peters, declared “that Congress have no objec tion to [such] a conference,” but the report was rejected on September 20 after a pro tracted debate that included four roll call votes and a number of proposed amend ments.
