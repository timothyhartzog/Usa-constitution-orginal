The Numbers run to as many more, the remainder are to form a second Vol. which will be published in a few weeks, & I will do myself the pleasure to send it to you as soon as it is done. 4 Mr. Madison, in a letter which he wrote me a few days ago, 5 re quested me to obtain the first & second volumes of the Philosophical Transactions of the society in Philada. & forward them to you. Upon enquiry I find that the 1st Vol. is not in print having been destroyed during the War. The second, I have got the favor of Mr. Barlow to take with him for you. I am told the 1st is to be reprinted. When it is done we will take care to forward it to you. During Mr. Madisons absence in Virginia I am aware of your dependance upon me for regular infor mation upon the progress of the business of the constitution and shall omit no opportunity of writing. I have the Honor to be, My dr. sir with the most sincere regard, Yr. Friend & Hl St., Ed.
