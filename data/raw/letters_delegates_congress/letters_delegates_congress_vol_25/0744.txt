John Fell’s Diary [September 11-16, 1780] Monday Sepr 11th. This day a number of Letters were read from J Adams which took up a deal of time and of little consequence. Letters from Genl. Washington and General Gates, and several others, Re- ferrd to Committee. 1 Tuesday September 12th, 1780. Letter from Genl Washington, Genl How and one from G1 Nixon to Resign on account of the Ill state of his health accepted. 2 A Long Letter from the Governor of the State of Vermont so called which took up a good deal of debate and the read ing of all the former Resolutions &c to be considerd on Thursday. 3 Wednesday Sepr 13th. After the dispatches were gone through, the New Plan for the Hospital department was began upon, and contin ued till adjournment. 4 Thursday Sepr 14th. Some memorials &c being Read the order of the day on the Vermont affair was taken up, and as usual a long debate. Friday Sepr. 15th. Some dispatches were read.
