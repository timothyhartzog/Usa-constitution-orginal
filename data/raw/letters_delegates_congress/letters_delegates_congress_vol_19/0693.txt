Virginia Delegates to Benjamin Harrison Sir, Philadelphia Feby. 4th. 1783 Neither this post nor the preceding one brought us any letter from your Excellency. The affair with Nathan is now under arbitration. There is a refer ence in the case sent us of a Certificate from Clarke & Todd that the Bills were negociated at the current depredation. But we find no such Certificate among the Papers transmitted to us, which is likely to turn the arbitration against the State. 1 Congress have under their Consideration the Papers we were directed to lay before them. 2 We have not receivd any material Intelligence since our last. We have the honor to be with the greatest respect, Yr. Excellencys most obedt. & most Huml. Servts. Jos. Jones. J. Madison Jr. Theok. Bland Jr. A. Lee RC (Vi: Continental Congress Papers). Written by Lee and signed by Lee, Bland, Jones, and Madison.
