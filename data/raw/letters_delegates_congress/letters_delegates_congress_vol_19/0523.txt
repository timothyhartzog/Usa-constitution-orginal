On December 18, a November 16 letter from Jacob Cuyler, former deputy commissary general of purchases, enclosing a November 8 letter from Tichenor, his former assistant, was read and referred to a committee chaired by Madison. Tichenor was being sued in New Hampshire for personal debts he incurred when purchasing provisions for the army. Congress decided to refer his case to that state for settlement.
