Daniel Carroll to William Paca Dear Sr Pha. Deer. 31st. 1782. Mr. Marbois calld yesterday with the enclosed. I was not at home but suppose it to be on public business & therefore inclose it under a Frank—if it should prove otherwise please to settle it with the post Office, i With great respect, &c I am yr Exys Mo Obt Sert, Dani Carroll 2 RC (MdAA: Red Books).
