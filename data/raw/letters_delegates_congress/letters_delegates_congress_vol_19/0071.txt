Philadelphia Augst. 7th. 1782 We have this Day had an Interview with the Minister of France at his Request; & are desired by him to communicate to your Excellency, his Sentiments respecting two Frenchmen, 1 who, on Account of their dis orderly Conduct in assisting to break open the Goal or Workhouse, (the particulars of which we are not well informed of) are held to answer for their Misdemeanor, before the Judicial Authority of the Commonwealth. The Right of exercising which Authority, the Minis ter informs us, has been call’d in Question by the Consul of his most Christian Majesty, as also by the Commodore in the Ha[r]bor of Boston.
