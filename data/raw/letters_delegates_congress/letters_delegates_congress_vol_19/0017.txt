Chrononlogy of Congress xiii September 6 September 9 September 10 September 12 September 14 September 16 September 17 September 19 & 20 September 24 September 28 October 1 October 3 October 10 October 11 October 14 & 15 Debates proposal to appeal to the states to cede western lands. Suspends issuance of bills of exchange to pay loan office certificate interest; instructs Wash ington on prisoner cartel. Sets state fiscal quotas. Endorses Robert Aitken’s proposal to print an American edition of the Bible. Authorizes solicitation of $4 million in foreign loans. Commissions Washington to negotiate prisoner exchange. Refuses to accept Henry Laurens’ resignation as peace commissioner. Debates report that Henry Laurens improperly petitioned parliament while imprisoned. Receives information from the chevalier de La Luzerne on recent peace maneuvers in Europe. Adopts plan of a treaty of amity and commerce with Sweden. Rejects New Jersey plan to retain Continental revenues for the payment of the state’s Conti nental troops. Reassures France on U.S. commitment to mili tary preparedness and to its no separate peace pledge. Appeals to Rhode Island and Georgia to adopt impost amendment. Sets day of thanksgiving and prayer. Debates promotion of general officers.
