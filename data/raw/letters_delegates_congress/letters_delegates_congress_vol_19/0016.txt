Chronology of Congress AUGUST 1, 1782-MARCH 11, 1783 August 1 Reorganizes adjutant general’s department. August 5 Receives Robert Morris’ funding plan. August 6 Revises John Jay’s diplomatic instructions. August 7 Reorganizes Continental Army. August 9 Receives British commissioners’ announcement that peace negotiations have begun at Paris. August 12 Authorizes Washington to negotiate prisoner exchange. August 14 Suspends inquiry into General Gates’ conduct at Camden. August 15 August 16 & 20 August 23 August 27 August 29 Rejects move to repeal peace commissioners’ instructions to be guided by French court. Debates Massachusetts’ petition to include fish eries claim in peace ultimata. Appoints judges to hear Connecticut-Pennsylva nia boundary dispute. Debates Kentucky statehood petition. Orders purchase of ship for packet service to Europe. September 3 Orders resumption of postal service to the Car olinas and Georgia; presents ship America to France. September 4 Sets fiscal quota for the immediate payment of interest on the public debt.
