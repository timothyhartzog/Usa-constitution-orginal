Abraham Clark to Robert Morris Sir, Septr 22nd [i.e. 23] 1782 1 You have enclosed for Perusal a Letter from Mr. Pollock. The Com mittee to whom the same is referred desire you will as soon as possible give them such a State of the Matters therein mentioned as your Means of Information may enable you to do. Tr (DNA: PCC, item 50) From the text copied into a letter from Robert Morris to James Duane of October 11, 1782. > Clark wrote this letter in behalf of a committee (consisting of himself, James Duane, and Hugh Williamson) appointed on September 23 to take into consideration a Septem ber 18 appeal from Oliver Pollock for the settlement of his accounts as commercial agent of the United States in New Orleans. Morris returned this copy of it (the only form in which it survives) to Duane, in the understanding that Duane was “the Chairman of that Committee,” with a number of enclosures explaining the claim and a recommenda tion for the speedy settlement of Pollock’s accounts. The date of Clark s letter is obvi ously in error, as September 22 was a Sunday and the committee was appointed on September 23. For Pollock’s letter, the committee’s appointment, and Morris’ response to Clark’s inquiry, see PCC, item 50, fols. 1-13, 17-26, item 186, fol. 58; and Morris, Papers (Ferguson), 6:560-63.
