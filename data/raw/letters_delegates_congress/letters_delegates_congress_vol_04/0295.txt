June 18, 1776 263 short Time since, & your Desire of being remembred to them shall be complied with. Things are going on well in the Colonies with Respect to In dependency, Confederation 8cc &c, Sc the Question relative to the former is to be agitated in Congress the 1st July next. General Washington is to be reinforced with 15000 men at New York which will augment his Army to 25000 8c a flying Camp is to be posted in the Jerseys consisting of 10000 men more. You have undoubtedly heard of the prize lately taken 8c carried into Boston out of which were landed seventy five Tons of Powder, 1000 Arms 8cc 8cc 8cc. Saltpetre is manufactured in Abundance; in the Massa chusetts only by Mr Devens Account they have already delivered into the Magazines fifty Tons, 8c have thirty Tons of Sulphur imported & left in Boston. Three Mills are built there two of which turn out upwards of 1000 each per Week. I hope the Disposition that has appeared in some officers to censure others will cease, 8c that in Leiu thereof a laudable Emulation will take place to excell in Discipline 8c Valour, without which an Army must be disgraced. I sincerely wish You success 8c happiness, 8c remain sir your Friend 8c hum ser, Elbridge Gerry P.S. Pray continue to give me the state of Things in Canada. Elbridge Gerry to Joseph Trumbull Dear sir, Philadelphia June 18th 1776 I received your Favour of the 10th Inst., since which Mr Carol 8c Chace have arrived here 8c given to Congress a lengthy Detail of the confused State of Things in that Department. To rectifie Mat ters, General Gates to the Disapointment of the eastern District is Ordered to the Command there, with suitable Powers to organize where wanting 8c reduce to order the Army in that Country. Congress see the Necessity of a full Enquiry into the Miscarriages that have taken place, 8c a Scrutiny will be made into the Causes of the Failure in the Lines ordered, as well as those of the Want of provisions, Ammunition, Cloathing 8c pay.
