James Lovell to Abigail Adams May 21. 1781 I hope you are not still without later Dates from Mr. A’s Hand, than what we have—Oct. 24. 1 I conclude he was well about the last of Febry. because Mr. Carmichael under Date of March 11th sends us Mr. A’s Plan of a Loan to be opened at the House of Nieufville & Son March 1st. 2 We have no Vessels from Holland. Accept of my Conjecture as a Proof of my uniform Wish to contribute to your Ease of Mind & genuine Happiness. I have sent an open 3plicate to Mr. A for Mr. Moylan respecting yr. Invoice. 3 Free from Pain, I shall however limp for many a day. RC (MHi: Adams Papers), In Lovell’s hand, though not signed.
