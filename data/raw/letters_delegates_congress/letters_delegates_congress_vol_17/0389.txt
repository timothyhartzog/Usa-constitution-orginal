June 27, 1781 355 Samuel Huntington to Thomas Barclay Sir, Philada June 27. 1781 By the enclosed Act of Congress you will be informed they have been pleased to appoint you Vice Consul to reside in France, with Authority to exercise all the Powers & perform the Services required from Wm. Palfrey Esquire, during his Absence from that Kingdom, ordering the Pleasure of Congress, and you are to be allowed a Salary of one thousand Dollars per Annum. 1 I am Sir &c &c, S.H. LB (DNA: PCC, item 16). 1 William Palfrey’s disappearance at sea en route to taking up his duties as consul in France the previous winter made it difficult for Congress to reach a decision on filling his post, and Thomas Barclay was accordingly named “Vice Consul” rather than Palfrey’s successor out of consideration for the former’s friends who retained a faint hope for his survival. Barclay (1728-93), a Philadelphia merchant of Irish background, immediately accepted the appointment, but before he departed for France in October he requested that he be appointed consul as Palfrey’s successor, sufficient time now having elapsed to dispel uncertainty over the latter’s disappearance, and Congress immediately complied with the request. See JCC, 20:687, 698, 716, 735-37, 21:1036; and PCC, item 78, 4:229-32, 257-60. For the origins of congressional interest in the appointment of a consul in France, see Huntington to Palfrey, November 8, 1780. For Barclay’s subsequent career abroad, see Adams, Diary (Butterfield), 3:120n; Morris, Papers (Ferguson), 3:263n.7; and JCC, 23:728-30, 773-82. Samuel Huntington to Certain States Sir, Philada June 27. 1781 The Regiment of Invalids being at the Desire of the Commander in Chief forthwith to be removed from this City to garrison West point on the North River, Congress have thought it expedient to pass the enclosed Resolve of the 26th Instant, recommending to the States of Delaware, Maryland & Virginia to settle with the Officers & Men of that Regiment who are Citizens of those States respectively on Certificates from the Commanding Officer without requiring personal Attendance to liquidate their Accounts for Depreciation. 1 I have the Honor to be &c &c, S.H. LB (DNA: PCC, item 16). Addressed: “To President Rodney. N.B. The like verbatim to the Governors of Maryland & Virginia.” 'See JCC, 20:697.
