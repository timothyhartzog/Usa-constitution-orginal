452 July 28, 1781 is a matter of the highest Consequence, that the requisitions of our worthy General on the different States be punctually & immediately Complied with. You could not do a more essential Service to these States, than to urge the completion of the levies for the Army. Lord Cornwallis has returned to Portsmouth and the Marquis after gaining great Laurels in his late attack on his Lordship is refreshing his little Army, so as at the same time to cover the Country. Could you with propriety communicate what you consider as the Ideas of our Legislature, relative to the dispute of Vermont & the expediency of their being declared a free & independent State, I should be much obliged.
