Virginia Delegates to Thomas Nelson Sir, Philadelphia, Augt. 21st. 1781. We had the Honour, yesterday, of writing to your Excellency by Mr. Nicolson; 1 since which no Intelligence of Importance hath transpired. By some Accident or other the Mail from Virginia did not arrive yesterday; and we are not favoured with any Accounts from that quarter. The inclosed Gazette will give you the News of the Day. We have the Honour to be with great Esteem, Yr. Excellency’s most obedt. & hble Servants. Jos. Jones. M. Smith 2 James Madison Junr. Edm. Randolph Theok. Bland RC (Vi: Continental Congress Papers). Written by Smith and signed by Smith, Bland, Jones, Madison, and Randolph. 1 Apparently George Nicolson, who had been sent to Philadelphia to purchase military supplies for state troops. See Virginia Delegates’ Memorandum, April 27, 1781, note 3. 2 This is the last delegate letter signed by Meriwether Smith, who obtained a leave of absence on August 31. He probably remained in Philadelphia for another month, however, for he was appointed to a committee on September 3 and later claimed £590.8 for 246 days service "from 28th Jany. 1781, to the 30th Sep. 1781 inclusive.” See JCC, 21:925, 930; and Continental Congress Papers, Vi.
