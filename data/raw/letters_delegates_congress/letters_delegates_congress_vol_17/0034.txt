As senator from Maryland in the first federal Congress, Carroll supported Alexander Hamilton’s plan for the assumption of state debts and worked to locate the national Capitol on the Potomac, near his own residence. He was named by President Washington one of the commissioners to survey the District of Columbia and served as commissioner of the district until his death in 1795. Engraving by Max Rosenthal.
