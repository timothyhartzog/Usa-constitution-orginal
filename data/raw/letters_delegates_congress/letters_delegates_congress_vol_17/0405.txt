My time is short I have only one moment to tell Fanny that I love her, that I long to receive one more letter from her fair hand—that my anxiety for her welfare has been perpetual 8c that nothing can relieve me but an assurance from her self that she is well and happy, at least as much so as the times will admit of. Bland RC (ViW: Tucker-Coleman Papers). 1 For the June 13 resolve of Congress to “make good the depreciation to the Medical Staff,” see Samuel Huntington to the States, June 15, 1781, note 1. Tucker’s brother, Dr. Thomas Tudor Tucker (1745-1828) of South Carolina, had been captured by the British and served as a physician and surgeon at the Charleston hospital. Following his exchange on June 9, 1781, he escorted prisoners to Wil liamsburg, Va., where he became director of the Continental hospital. He was not officially appointed to the Continental service, however, until September 1781. He later served in the South Carolina assembly and became a delegate to Congress in 1787-88.
