# Complete Document Catalog

## Repository Overview

This document provides a comprehensive catalog of all original primary and secondary source materials in the Constitutional Research System, organized by category and chronological period.

**Total Documents**: 18 text files
**Time Period Covered**: 1787-1791 (with some correspondence extending to 1789)
**Total Word Count**: ~200,000+ words

---

## I. The Constitution (1787)

### 1. US Constitution - 1787
- **File**: `us_constitution_1787.txt`
- **Document Type**: Official constitutional text
- **Date**: September 17, 1787 (adoption date)
- **Source**: Project Gutenberg
- **Content**: The complete original text of the United States Constitution, including preamble, seven articles, and signature section
- **Key Details**:
  - Original constitutional framework
  - Establishes federal structure and separation of powers
  - Pre-Bill of Rights version
  - Includes all articles on legislative, executive, and judicial branches
- **Word Count**: ~4,400 words
- **Primary Use Cases**:
  - Understanding the original constitutional framework
  - Comparing to amendments
  - Studying the founding structure of government

- **Source**: Project Gutenberg

  - Ratification strategies and persuasion
- **Word Count**: ~45,000+ words
- **Major Sections**:
  - Papers 1-14: General arguments for union and Constitution
  - Papers 15-22: Defects of the existing system
  - Papers 23-36: Powers necessary for the Union
  - Papers 37-51: Structure of the Constitution
  - Papers 52-84: Detailed examination of provisions
  - Papers 85: Concluding remarks
- **Primary Use Cases**:
