# Federalism in the Founders' Own Words

The debate over the proper balance between the federal government and the states is older than the Constitution itself. The 1787 Convention met in part because the Articles of Confederation had given too little power to the central government; the ratification debate was largely a fight over whether the new Constitution gave it too much.
