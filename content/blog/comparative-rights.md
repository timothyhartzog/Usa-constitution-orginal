# Comparing Rights Across 194 Constitutions

The U.S. Bill of Rights was once unusual. Two centuries later, almost every national constitution names a similar list of fundamental rights. But the wording, scope, and exceptions vary wildly. This post uses the platform's live search and comparison widgets to explore those differences.
